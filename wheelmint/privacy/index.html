Wheelmint – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short: Wheelmint does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and works without an internet connection.

1. Who we are

The app is published by İdil Sakıncı, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: idilsakinci2@gmail.com.

2. About the app

Wheelmint is a spin-the-wheel decision maker: you add your own options, spin the wheel and the app picks one at random.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app. The options that you type are used only to run the wheel on your device and are not sent anywhere.

  • No account, e-mail address, phone number or name is requested.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools are included.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • The app reads your device's language setting only to choose the interface language; this value stays on your device.

4. Data stored on your device

Anything the app keeps (for example your chosen language or the current session's content) stays on your device. Removing the app removes this data.

5. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may process information about your download, purchase or optional diagnostics under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal data from them.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we hold no data about children or anyone else.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold data about you, or have any question, write to idilsakinci2@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK) you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Because data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption, OS updates).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

idilsakinci2@gmail.com
