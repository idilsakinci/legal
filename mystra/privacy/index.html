Mystra: Horoscope & Tarot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short: Mystra needs an account (e-mail, name, birth details) to personalize your horoscope. We use this data only to run the App, we never sell it, and we show no ads and use no advertising or cross-app tracking. You can delete your account and all its data at any time inside the App.

1. Controller

İdil Sakıncı, an individual developer ("we", "us"), is the data controller. Contact: idilsakinci2@gmail.com.

We do not collect your precise or approximate device location (the birth place is text you type), contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not use advertising identifiers.

3. On your device

The App stores your language choice, cached daily content and notification settings on your device. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your device; no push-notification service or token is used. You can disable it in the App or in your device settings.

4. Legal bases (GDPR) / hukuki sebepler (KVKK)

  • Providing the service you request, i.e. performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK m. 5/2-c).
  • Security, fraud and abuse prevention - our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK m. 5/2-f).
  • Notifications - your consent given through the device permission prompt and the in-app setting (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligations where applicable (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK m. 5/2-ç).

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising. We use service providers (processors) strictly to operate the App: cloud hosting, a managed database and a transactional e-mail service used to send password-reset codes. They process data only on our instructions. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own (including the EEA, the United Kingdom or the United States). Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

7. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, password-reset records and support messages are permanently deleted from our database immediately. Encrypted provider backups may keep copies for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) until they are overwritten. Server logs are kept for a short period for security and are then deleted.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You may access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, request a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. In the App: Profile → Edit Profile to correct data, and Profile → Delete Account to delete everything. You can also write to idilsakinci2@gmail.com from your registered e-mail address; we reply within 30 days. See also how to delete your account. You may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text; authenticated actions require a signed session token; sensitive endpoints are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Mystra is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

12. Contact

idilsakinci2@gmail.com
